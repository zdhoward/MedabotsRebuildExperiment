## The Medal: eight skills, one gauge, three breakpoints

A Medal is more than a nameplate. It carries an overall Medal Level (progression
trigged by Robattle experience), **eight independent Skill levels** (0-100, one
per action type: Strike, Berserk, Shoot, Snipe, Defend, Heal, Support, Interrupt),
an Attribute that gates part compatibility, a compatibility bonus that bleeds
into ROS/Evasion/Defense calculations, and a Target/Aim bias that steers
CPU/rotation targeting [DOCUMENTED — S-M, S-S, S-K, S-R].

Medaforce is the chargeable resource (max **80**) that fuels special Medal
abilities. It charges **+40** per dedicated charge action or **+20** per damage
taken. Abilities unlock at Medal levels **10** (first), **30** (second), **60**
(third), with Kabuto/Kuwagata gaining a fourth *form-only* transformation at
**100** with no additional Medaforce [DOCUMENTED — S-K, S-R].

## The targeting tie-in

Medal Target/Aim is Medarot 2 CORE's simplified Medal AI: it is a Skill-type
preference that biases which enemy parts get targeted when a Medabot chooses
an action [DOCUMENTED — S-R]. This is a *targeting priority modifier*, not
a full AI brain — a critical distinction from earlier Medarot games that
used richer personality systems [DOCUMENTED — S-R].

## Medaforce: the bypass mechanic

Normal offensive Medaforce attacks **bypass** Dodge, Part Defense, and Guard
effects [DOCUMENTED — S-R]. Status Medaforces still respect target
immunity/resistance, making them a partial counter rather than a guaranteed
win button [DOCUMENTED — S-R]. This is why Medaforce attacks function as
hard counters to defensive builds in the late game.

The power formula for many Medaforces is:

```
Medaforce Result = Base Medaforce Power + (Compatibility Bonus / 8) + (relevant Medal Skill / 8)
```

**40 Skill contributes +5**, **100 Skill contributes +12.5** (integer handling
TBD) [COMMUNITY-TESTED — S-R].

## The compatibility conflict surfaces again

This is the second spec to encounter the Medapedia-vs-Guide contradiction.
Kenshin_Xtreme's walkthrough states Tortoise/Jellyfish/Bear medals give **+1**
compatibility bonus. The owner-supplied compiled report claims **+7** for
those same medals. Kabuto/Kuwagata at **+4** matches both sources, so the
breakpoint is the non-starter medals.

**T0.5 position:** The *existence* of compatibility bonuses is DOCUMENTED
(rules 11-15). The *magnitude* is UNVERIFIED and **must not enter data/ or
the engine** until disassembly or in-game measurement resolves it. This is
now a **blocking item** for T0.7 data schema (which needs compatibility
fields) and T0.8 seed dataset (which needs actual values).

The conflict is recorded in BACKLOG.md [2026-09-23] and cross-referenced in
both battle-flow.md (T0.2) and damage-and-success.md (T0.4).

## Falsifiable predictions

- **Skill progression:** Use a Strike-type part 8 times in succession -> Strike
  Skill level increases by 1 (rules 7-8).
- **Gauge math:** Start at 0 MF -> charge once -> 40 MF. Take damage -> 60
  MF. Charge again -> 80 MF (capped, rule 26).
- **Unlock gate:** Medal at Level 9 cannot use *any* Medaforce; at Level 10
  the first Medaforce becomes available (rules 19, 27, 30).
- **Bypass:** An 80-MF offensive Medaforce with sufficient gauge succeeds
  against a target with 200 Dodge and 200 Part Defense (rule 31).
- **Compatibility:** Same Medabot with all-matching parts vs all-non-matching
  parts shows measurable ROS/Evasion/Defense delta (rules 12-13).
