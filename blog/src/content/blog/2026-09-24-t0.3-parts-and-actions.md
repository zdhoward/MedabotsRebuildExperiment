## What the sources say

Medapedia's [Stats](https://medarot.meowcorp.us/wiki/Stats) page (S-2) is the
primary reference for part composition. It states a Medabot has **4 parts**: Head +
Right Arm + Left Arm + Legs, with Head parts having **7 stats** (Armor, ROS, Power,
Uses, Skill, Action, Attribute) and Arm parts having **8 stats** (adds Charge and
Radiation). Leg parts have **7 stats**: Armor, Propulsion, Evasion, Defense, Proximity,
Remoteness, and Leg type.

The same page lists the **8 active skill categories**: Strike, Berserk, Shoot, Aim
Shot, Defend, Heal, Support, Interrupt. Kenshin_Xtreme's guide (S-4) corroborates
these and adds the English terminology mapping (e.g., Reckless = Berserk, Snipe = Aim
Shot).

Medapedia's [Leg type](https://medarot.meowcorp.us/wiki/Leg_type) page (S-3) documents
the **7 leg types**: Bipedal, Multi-leg, Wheeled, Tank, Hover/Float, Flying, Aquatic.

## The action taxonomy

The spec's Action Taxonomy Table documents **41 base action types** organized by
skill:
- **Strike (2)**: Sword, Destroy
- **Berserk (2)**: Hammer, Chain Reaction
- **Shoot (9)**: Rifle, Gatling/Chain Gun, Laser, Beam, Missile, Napalm, Anti-Air, Anti-Sea, Sacrifice
- **Aim Shot (1)**: Snipe
- **Defend (5)**: Defense, Half Block, Full Block, Counter, Cross Attack setup
- **Heal (6)**: Recovery, Repair, Reactivate, AutoRecover, Status Clear, Trap Clear
- **Support (4)**: Scout, Conceal, Boost Charge, Rapid Charge
- **Interrupt (11)**: Disable, Confusion, No Escape, No Defense, Charge Drain, Ammo Drain, Force Drain, Force Bind, Pushover, Transform

Kenshin_Xtreme (S-4) is the sole source for several action entries (Chain Reaction,
Half Block, Full Block, Counter, Cross Attack setup, AutoRecover, Status Clear, Trap
Clear, Boost Charge, Rapid Charge, No Escape, No Defense, Charge Drain, Ammo Drain,
Force Drain, Force Bind, Pushover). Where only one community guide states it, I marked
it DOCUMENTED against that guide and left it to T0.8 to verify against primary data.

The attribute taxonomy lists **24 compatibility categories** drawn from Medapedia's
Stats page (S-2) and Leg type page (S-3), including Grapple, Optic, Bomb, Anti-Air,
Anti-Sea.

## Where the evidence is thin

Seven open questions emerged that the spec explicitly refuses to invent:

1. **Head internal CRG/RAD**: Heads do not display Charge/Radiation (DOCUMENTED —
   S-2), but whether they have internal values is UNKNOWN.

2. **Success formula**: How ROS, Evasion, Defense, and randomness combine —
   UNVERIFIED, T0.4 scope.

3. **Aim weighting**: How Medal Target/Aim biases target selection — UNKNOWN,
   no source publishes the algorithm.

4. **Leg stat formulas**: How Propulsion, Evasion, Defense, Proximity, Remoteness
   numerically affect CRG/RAD/damage/success — UNVERIFIED, T0.4 scope.

5. **Leg x terrain matrix**: Complete bonus/penalty values — UNKNOWN, T0.6 scope.

6. **Head Uses mid-battle restoration**: Whether Heal actions can restore head
   Uses — UNKNOWN.

7. **Berserk vulnerability**: Exact evasion/defense penalty values — HYPOTHESIS
   only (tiomasta's thread, S-7).

## One verified formula

Kenshin_Xtreme (S-4) documents the Berserk damage formula:
**Effective Berserk Power = Part Power + (Propulsion / 2)**.

This is the only exact formula in the spec. I recorded it verbatim and flagged it
as DOCUMENTED — S-4. It will be a primary test oracle for T0.4's damage formula work:
a Berserk action with Power=100 and Propulsion=50 should deal **125 base damage**.
