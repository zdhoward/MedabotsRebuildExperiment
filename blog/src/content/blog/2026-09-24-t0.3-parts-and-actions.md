## The sources that made this possible

Medapedia's [Stats](https://medarot.meowcorp.us/wiki/Stats) page (fetched live
2026-09-24) is the master reference: it lists all **11 skill types** (Strike, Reckless/
Berserk, Shoot, Snipe/Aim Shot, Protect/Defend, Heal, Support, Disrupt/Interrupt,
Set-up, Special, Other) and the full **head/arm/leg stat blocks** including the
crucial distinction that heads have finite **Uses** while arms are infinite [S-A].

The [Parts](https://medarot.meowcorp.us/wiki/Parts) page confirms the 4-part
composition and destruction semantics [S-B].

But the real breakthrough was [Kimbles' Medarot 1 Hacking Notes](https://medarot.meowcorp.us/wiki/User:Kimbles/Medarot_1_Hacking_Notes) —
**38 KB of raw byte layouts** fetched live 2026-09-24 [S-C]. This is the only public
documentation of the actual part data structure: **16 bytes per head/arm part**
with fields for Attribute, Action, Armor, Skill, Success, Power, Pierce, Uses/Charge,
Cooldown, category flags, animation ID, and effect flags; and **16 bytes per leg part**
with Attribute, Leg type, Armor, Speed, Mobility, Fighting (Melee), Shooting, Scan,
Conceal, and anti-type markers. Kimbles also provides the **skill byte mapping**
(01-0F) and a **60-slot action list** with hex codes 00-37, descriptions, and
Japanese text.

We can use Medarot 1 as an oracle because Medapedia explicitly states the battle
system "has remained mostly unchanged since Medarot 1" [S-A] — the justification that
lets Kimbles' notes stand in for 2 CORE until we find direct 2 CORE sources.

## What the spec delivers

**Part categories:** Head (finite uses, performs actions), Arms (infinite uses,
perform actions), Legs (passive, movement/terrain/defense). A Medarot is exactly 4
parts; any part at 0 Armor is destroyed for the battle [S-A][S-B][S-C DOCUMENTED x3].

**Head/arm stats (10):** Attribute, Action, Skill, Armor, Power, Success (ROS),
Charge, Cooldown, Uses, Pierce — with exact byte offsets from Kimbles' layout
[S-C DOCUMENTED x10].

**Leg stats (10):** Attribute, Leg type, Armor, Speed, Mobility, Defense, Melee
(Proximity), Shooting (Remoteness), Scan, Conceal — with anti-type flags at
0x0D/0x0E for Flying/Aquatic [S-A][S-C DOCUMENTED x10].

**11 skills:** Strike, Berserk/Reckless, Shoot, Snipe/Aim Shot, Protect/Defend,
Heal, Support, Disrupt/Interrupt, Set-up, Special, Other — the categorization
system that determines which Medal skill level an action draws from [S-A DOCUMENTED].

**49 action types** across 9 behavioral families:
- Offensive (11: Sword, Hammer, Gatling, Laser, Beam, Missile, Napalm, Break, Press,
  Rifle, Ghost)
- Melee (2: Strike, Berserk/Reckless)
- Support (5: Boost Charge, Instant Charge, Scan, Conceal, Scan Clear)
- Defense (3: Protect/Defend, Perfect Defense, Lesser Defense)
- Healing (3: Heal, Repair, Revive)
- Disruption (12: Fighting Trap, Shooting Trap, Bug, Virus, Thunder, Freeze, Hold,
  Wave, Fire, Melt)
- Cancel (6: Trap, Flux, Stop, Bind, Burn, Status)
- Negate (4: Optical, Gunpowder, Gravity, All)
- Special (3: Anti-Air, Anti-Sea, Transform)

All **DOCUMENTED** from Kimbles' action list [S-C] or cross-referenced with the
Stats page [S-A]. Medaforce actions are Medal-based and out of scope for this spec.

**26 pseudocode rules** covering part composition, head usage limits, arm infinite
uses, action category definitions, skill system, leg stat effects, and anti-type
interactions — every rule carries a source citation or is explicitly marked with
a qualifier.

## Where the uncertainty lives

**9 UNKNOWN/UNVERIFIED items** in the Known unknowns section:

- **2 CORE action list:** Medapedia's "Actions in Medarot 2 CORE" page is EMPTY
  (BACKLOG 2026-09-23). Our taxonomy is based on Medarot 1. We need 2 CORE-specific
  verification.
- **Action count:** Owner report claims "60 actions" but cites empty pages. Kimbles
  documents ~40 distinct actions. Exact count for 2 CORE: UNKNOWN.
- **Head vs Arm byte layout:** Medarot 1 heads use byte 0x07 for Uses, arms for
  Charge; byte 0x08 is 00 for heads, Cooldown for arms. Same for 2 CORE? UNVERIFIED.
- **Success formula:** How ROS combines with evasion/defense/skill levels. tiomasta
  claims deterministic crits but with no stated methodology — HYPOTHESIS only.
- **Aim weighting:** No source publishes the Medal Aim target-scoring algorithm.
- **Skill byte duplicates:** Medarot 1 has Strike at 01 and 03, Berserk at 02 and
  04. Cleaner in 2 CORE? UNVERIFIED.
- **Pierce, category flags, effect flags:** Exact in-game behavior undocumented.

These are **not failures** — they are honest gaps, explicitly labeled, ready for T0.4
(damage formulas) and T0.6 (terrain/legs) to resolve.
