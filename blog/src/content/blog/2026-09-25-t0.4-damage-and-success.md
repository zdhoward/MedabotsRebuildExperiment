## The tiomasta problem

tiomasta's GameFAQs thread [S-B] is the *only* source publishing actual damage
formulas for Medabots GBA. It claims: Evasion and Defense each subtract 25% of
their value from damage (F2), ROS adds 25% of itself as bonus damage (F1),
and crits are deterministic ROS vs (Evasion + Defense) with a small random
factor (F4). **Methodology: unstated.** Every formula from this source is
therefore HYPOTHESIS-grade until disassembly or in-game measurement confirms
it.

This is the core tension in T0.4: we need formulas to build the engine, but
the only formulas available are community-tested without documented methods.
The spec therefore explicitly labels each formula's provenance and provides
falsifiable test cases for future verification.

## What's verified vs. hypothesized

**DOCUMENTED (4 rules):**

- Leader/partner head destruction outcomes (rules 17-18) [battle-flow T0.2]

**HYPOTHESIS (11 rules, 7 formulas):**
- F1: BaseDamage = Power + (Power × SkillLevel/100) + (Success × 0.25)
- F2: DamageReduction = (Evasion × 0.25) + (Defense × 0.25); FinalDamage = max(1, BaseDamage - DamageReduction)
- F3: Hit if Success >= Evasion
- F4: Crit if Success >= Evasion + Defense
- F6: Excess damage passes through to another part on destruction
- F7: Recovery-turn (RAD phase) attack-type weaknesses exist
- F9: Anti-Air vs Flying and Anti-Sea vs Aquatic deal 2× damage

**UNVERIFIED (3 rules):**
- Crit damage multiplier (assumed 2×, community convention)
- Passthrough priority (which part absorbs excess damage)
- Recovery-turn weakness specifics (which types, multipliers)

## The compatibility conflict

Medapedia documents that Medal-part Attribute compatibility exists [S-A].
Kenshin_Xtreme's guide states Tortoise/Jellyfish/Bear medals give **+1** stat
bonus. The owner-supplied compiled report claims **+7** for the same medals.
This is a **direct conflict** recorded in BACKLOG.md [2026-09-23]. Until
disassembly or in-game measurement resolves this, **do not implement** the
magnitude in the engine. The existence of compatibility is DOCUMENTED; the
bonus value is UNVERIFIED.
